# EURO 2028 qualifying: draw facts

Inputs for `nt_euro2028_facts()` and `nt_read_euro2028_draw()` (`R/nt-euro2028.R`) and the draw simulator (`R/nt-euro2028-draw.R`). Team names are UEFA's spelling; `nt_uefa_to_martj42()` maps the three that differ from the martj42 results file (Türkiye, Czechia, Bosnia-Herzegovina).

| File | Content | Source (read 2026-11-24) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `seeding.csv` | The 54 entrants: draw pot, `nl_rank` (interim overall 2026/27 Nations League ranking, the 1 to 54 numbering of the pot list), Nations League league, group and group position, `nl_knockout` (`QF` quarter-finalist, `AB_PO` / `BC_PO` League A/B or B/C play-off in March 2027), `host` (the four final-tournament hosts) | UEFA, *Qualifying draw procedure, European Qualifiers 2026-28*, page 4 (PDF created 17 Nov 2026, <https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/02ab-221968265425-d5c7eee03b62-1000/eq_draw_procedure.pdf>); group placings from UEFA.com, "2026/27 Nations League: Quarter-final qualification, promotion, play-offs and relegation" (20 Nov 2026), which lists every group's final order and who enters the League A/B play-offs (League A's two lowest-ranked third-placed and two highest-ranked fourth-placed teams, League B's runners-up) and the League B/C play-offs. The pot order and the league placings agree: ranks 1-4 are the League A group winners, 5-8 the runners-up, and so on. |
| `quarter_finals.csv` | The four Nations League quarter-finals, numbered in UEFA's listing order; the first-named side hosts the first leg | UEFA.com, "UEFA Nations League quarter-final and play-off ties: Spain vs France, Norway vs England" (draw of 20 Nov 2026): two-legged ties in March 2027, "Teams listed second were seeded and will play the second legs at home". The QF numbers are ours; the draw-night loader also accepts a placeholder written by pairing (`Spain/France winner`). |
| `draw_conditions.csv` | `separate` (hosts and Northern Ireland, one per group), `winter` (at most two per group), `prohibited` pairs (never together), `travel` pairs (at most one such pair per group) | Draw procedure, pages 5-7. A condition on a quarter-finalist applies to both placeholders of its tie: the procedure says so for the prohibited pair (Spain, page 6) and the travel pairs (page 7); for the winter list (Norway) and host separation (England) it is our reading, the only one under which the condition holds whoever wins the tie. |

Other draw conditions, from the same procedure (pages 3, 5 and 8) and Article 13.02 of the 2026-28 regulations, are coded in `R/nt-euro2028-draw.R`: groups A-F have four teams and G-L five; pot 5 goes to G-L; the four quarter-final winner placeholders go to different groups of four; each group of five holds at most one quarter-final defeated placeholder or League A/B or B/C play-off team; each pot is emptied in turn, each drawn team going to the first group in alphabetical order that keeps every condition and the rest of the draw possible.

On draw night (6 December 2026) UEFA's groups go in `groups.csv` here (`group,pot,team`), and the fixture list, once published, in `fixtures_2027.csv` (`group,match_date,home_team,away_team,venue`); see `docs/runbooks/euro2028-draw-night.md`.
